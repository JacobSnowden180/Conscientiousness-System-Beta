Your perfect day, planned the night before.

You decide the night before, give each task a deadline, and follow the list.

How it works: Task is what you'll do, By Time is when it's due, and Status is Yes, No, or Late. Status is for reference only in the beta.

There are two ways to start. Build It Yourself starts with one blank row that you drag into order. Guided Setup asks about your wake time, bedtime, work, commute, meals, shower, and extras like pets or a workout, then suggests tasks you drag in. You can skip at any step.

How the beta works: up to 81 tasks, tasks and times move separately like a spreadsheet, and nothing is saved.

Why: most people do their best thinking two to four hours after they wake up, and planning the night before protects those hours.
