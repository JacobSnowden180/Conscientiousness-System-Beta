About the Conscientiousness System (Beta)

Your perfect day, planned the night before.

This started as a personal system: a yellow legal-pad schedule, printed out each day, with every task in a column, the time it's due by, and an honest mark for how it went. It works because it's simple. You decide the night before what matters, give each thing a deadline, and the next day you just follow the list.

How it works

Task: what you're going to do.

By (Time): the time it has to be done by.

Status: Yes, No, or Late with the time you finished. In this beta, Status is shown for reference only.

Two ways to start

Build It Yourself: start with one blank row, add tasks, type a time, and drag each column into order.

Guided Setup: answer a few quick questions (wake time, bedtime, work days and hours, commute, meals, shower, and extras like pets or a workout) and we'll suggest tasks with due-by times you can drag into your day. You can skip to Build It Yourself at any step.

How the beta works

Up to 81 tasks per day.

Tasks and times move independently, like a spreadsheet. A task pairs with whatever time is on its row.

Nothing is saved. Refresh the page to start fresh.

Why

Most people do their best thinking two to four hours after they wake up. Planning the night before protects those hours for the work that matters, instead of spending them deciding what to do.
